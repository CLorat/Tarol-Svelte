# Feedback from Tatalu (first consumer application)

Tatalu (`CLorat/Tatalu`) builds its SPA on the published packages `0.1.0-preview.5` (`tarol-client`, `tarol-svelte`,
`tarol-components`, `tarol-design`, `tarol-oidc`, `tarol-codegen`). Its foundation (Tatalu PR #1) showed the items below.
Ids `TS-*`; the backend side is in `CLorat/Tarol-Net` `docs/feedback/tatalu.md` (`TN-*`).

Priority: **P0** blocks Tatalu MVP stories; **P1** Tatalu ships with a workaround; **P2** nice to have.

| # | Pri | Change | Why (Tatalu) | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TS-1 | P1, closed: #46 | **`AppShell` navigation on phones.** With `nav` the shell is a two-column grid (`minmax(10rem, 14rem) 1fr`) at every width; at 360 px the page keeps about 200 px. *Checked: this part does not hold — below 40rem the shell was already one column (360 px main landmark, measured; `docs/migration/tatalu-feedback.md` TS-1); the bottom bar was missing.* Proposal: below a breakpoint the nav becomes a bar (top or bottom, thumb-reachable), or a `navPosition="bottom"` option. | Tatalu is mobile first (one hand, ≤ 2 taps, NFR-UX-01) and uses `AppShell` without `nav` plus its own bottom navigation (`apps/web/src/lib/BottomNav.svelte`). | `tarol-components/src/lib/AppShell.svelte` |
| TS-2 | P1, closed: #46 | **Message fallback for `<Aggregate>.NotFound`.** A view of a missing aggregate answers `ChildDayCare.NotFound`; the translator looks up that id, then `error.generic`.
